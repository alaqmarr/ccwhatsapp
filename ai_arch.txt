# 3. CONFIRMED PROBLEMS FROM ACTUAL AI LOGS

We have actual AI flight logs demonstrating several failures.

These are observed symptoms, not automatically confirmed code-level root causes.

Investigate the underlying implementation.

## Problem A — Unnecessary product retrieval

WhatsApp triggered product lookup for messages including:

- "Hi"
- "Who are you?"
- "I have a different query"
- Company information questions

Product search should only execute when relevant to the customer intent.

## Problem B — Conversation history contaminates searches

For an incoming "Hi", the backend constructed a search query containing old messages about pulpits and previous greetings.

Example:

"I am looking for pulpits under ₹25,000 I said I am looking for pulpits Who are yo Hi Hi"

This resulted in unrelated product recommendations.

Never construct search queries by blindly concatenating raw conversation messages.

Use structured active requirements and the current message.

## Problem C — Product category confusion

Customer:

"I am looking for pulpits under ₹25,000 for church events and premium functions."

ACRIO correctly identifies acrylic podiums.

Customer:

"We can increase the budget to ₹30,000."

ACRIO incorrectly searches Acrylic Sheets.

It should preserve the existing product category and change only the budget.

## Problem D — Product type mismatch

Customer:

"I am not looking for any tools, just machines."

The retrieval layer still returned records classified as `CONSUMABLE`.

The final answer sometimes filters them out, but strict constraints should be applied before product recommendations reach the model.

## Problem E — Irrelevant continuation

Customer:

"Who are you?"

ACRIO introduces itself but then continues recommending polishing machines and asking technical questions.

It should answer the actual question without forcing the previous product discussion into every response.

## Problem F — Inappropriate handoff

Customer:

"Whom will you connect me to?"

The system initiated staff handoff.

Distinguish informational questions about staff from explicit requests for handoff.

## Problem G — Large prompt-cache writes

A website request recorded approximately 35,171 cache-write tokens with zero cache-read tokens.

Investigate whether excessive static information or catalog content is being sent and whether prompt caching is effective across requests.

A single cache write is not necessarily a bug. Measure actual repeated-request behavior before changing it.

## Problem H — Cross-business retrieval risk

Some searches scan products from both Classic Concepts and Classic Edge 53.

Ensure that product searches are appropriately scoped to the requested business and category.

Do not allow unrelated machinery to appear in finished acrylic-product recommendations.
